## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, luminous dark interface tailored for a high-end web engineering agency. Moving away from muddy, low-contrast navy undertones, the system balances hyper-clarity with deep, polished obsidian tones. The visual identity unites Swiss-level typographic precision with subtle glassmorphism and radiant electric accents.

### Aesthetic Persona & Tone
- **Precision & Authority:** Structured layouts, rigorous alignment, and intentional spatial rhythm convey deep technical mastery and enterprise reliability.
- **Luminosity & Clarity:** Deep slate and rich obsidian backgrounds establish a cinematic stage where crisp text, translucent cards, and neon-cyan signals emerge with zero eye fatigue.
- **Architectural Minimalism:** Eliminates decorative noise in favor of luminous hierarchy, razor-sharp outlines, and soft atmospheric backdrops.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to eliminate muddy legibility issues through disciplined tonal separation between canvases, cards, and text tiers.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0a0f18` to `#0b1320`):** Obsidian slate foundation that retains warmth and depth without descending into pitch black or lifeless murky navy.
- **Card Surface (`#131d2e` at 85% opacity):** Frosted slate container elevated above the canvas, ensuring immediate perimeter visibility.
- **Card Surface Elevated (`#1b273d`):** Higher-tier containers for popovers, flyouts, and active states.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#223249`):** Precise delineation lines with 40–80% opacity, providing structure without visual clutter.

### Accents & Illumination
- **Primary Cyan (`#00f2fe` / `#06b6d4`):** High-frequency primary tone reserved for conversion actions, key highlights, and active telemetry signals.
- **Electric Indigo (`#6366f1`):** Secondary depth anchor for gradients, structural highlights, and secondary tags.
- **Sky Highlight (`#38bdf8`):** Tertiary balance for interactive hovers and informative callouts.

### Typography Contrast Spectrum
- **Primary Text (`#ffffff`):** Uncompromised 100% white for titles, headline numerals, and focal points.
- **Body Text (`#cbd5e1`):** High-contrast cool silver ensuring effortless reading over prolonged sessions.
- **Muted & Metadata (`#94a3b8`):** Crisp neutral slate for secondary metadata, subtext, and inactive UI elements.

## Typography

The type system pairs structural, technical modernism with fluid human readability:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides confident geometric cadence, sharp angles, and subtle engineering flair. Tight negative letter-spacing maintains compact density at larger scales.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced proportions, open counters, and humanist curves to ensure sustained reading comfort against dark backgrounds.
- **Labels & Overlines (JetBrains Mono):** Injects authentic developer and studio credibility, creating rapid recognition for technical specs, category chips, metrics, and dates.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive layout anchors all marketing showcases, service grids, and dashboards:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max container width of 1280px with 64px (`margin-lg`) side margins and 32px (`gutter-lg`) column gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column configuration with 32px (`margin-md`) margins and 24px (`gutter`) column gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`margin`) margins and 16px element gaps.

Vertical flow follows an 8px modular cadence. Hero sections and transitions use generous breathing space (`space-3xl`) to establish hierarchy, while interface cards employ balanced internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through controlled translucency, ambient colored blooms, and razor-sharp border definition instead of heavy mud-tinted drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep slate `#0b1320` enriched with low-opacity radial ambient gradients (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` to `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.05)`) positioned strategically behind key product modules.
- **Level 1 (Standard Card):** Background `#131d2e` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Surrounded by a 1px solid hairline border in `#223249`. Shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active State):** Background `#182337` with border transition to `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)` or `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`. Shadow: `0 16px 40px -12px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.12), 0 8px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Background `#1a263c` at 95% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on generous modern rounding that softens technical precision without sacrificing architectural tension:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.75rem` (12px) border radius.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) border radius.
- **Feature Modules & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) border radius.
- **Status Pills & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) for quick visual differentiation against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Linear gradient fill from `#00f2fe` to `#06b6d4`, high-contrast obsidian text (`#080e1a`) in font weight 600. Glowing halo on hover: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Translucent fill `rgba(34, 50, 73, 0.5)` with hairline border `#223249` and pure white text (`#ffffff`). On hover: background `#1f2d42`, border `#38bdf8`.
- **Indigo Accent:** Electric indigo fill `#6366f1` with white text for secondary focal actions and pipeline triggers.

### Cards & Modules
- Glassmorphic slate tiles (`#131d2e` at 85% opacity) framed with an ultra-fine border (`#223249`). 
- Inner header tags use `label-sm` in JetBrains Mono. 
- Top-right corner anchors support dynamic indicator pips (e.g., active cyan status dot with pulse ring).

### Input Fields
- Dark recessed background `#0c1322` with a 1px border `#223249` and `0.75rem` radius.
- Text entered in `#ffffff`; placeholder in `#64748b`.
- Focus state triggers a vibrant cyan border (`#00f2fe`) paired with a subtle outer cyan glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.15)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`) containers in `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.08)` fill with a 1px border in `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.25)`.
- Label styled in JetBrains Mono, uppercase, letter-spaced, colored in `#00f2fe`.
- Indigo variant: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)` fill with `#818cf8` text.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Default state in `#0f172a` with border `#334155`. Checked state fills with `#00f2fe`, delivering an obsidian checkmark or dot for distinct visual feedback.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Items separated by subtle divider lines (`#1b273d`). Hovering activates a smooth transition to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`. 
- Timestamps and technical parameters align to the right in `label-sm` (`#94a3b8`).
